        New job

        How it works
        • Queued: saved to data/agent.json. A run starts within a minute or two (an hourly run, 8 AM to midnight, catches anything missed).
        • Working: Claude has split it across subagents. Progress lines show up here live. Tell Claude redirects it before its next step; Stop makes it wrap up and save what it has; Open live session lets you watch or step in directly.
        • Files & links: encrypted on your device before saving, so they stay private even though the repo is public. Results from jobs with files are private too.
        • Needs you: it hit a login, a connector to approve, or a choice only you can make. Answer here and it goes back in the queue. You also get a push notification.
        • Done: tap Read to see the result. Results live in agent/results/.
        • Class work: Claude preps you (crash sheets, outlines, feedback on your drafts, practice problems). It won't do graded work or submit anything as you.
        • This repo is public. Titles, instructions, mid-run notes and results of jobs without files are public, so put anything private in a file or the links box. Never attach passwords.
