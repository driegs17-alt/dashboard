# Deco irrigation lamp: idea & market research

## Bottom line
**TEST, don't build yet. Confidence: medium.** Nobody sells a lamp that is self-watering, has a grow light and is styled Art Deco or Nouveau. Deco is trending, and the closest product (Soltech Aura, a $250 decorative grow lamp that doesn't water) has sold out three times. Two things make it hard. A batch of 25 would cost about **$183 a lamp**, which is only about a 20% margin at $295. And water next to power means safety certification. Do a 3-week waitlist + deposit test before you spend more than about $150 on hardware.
